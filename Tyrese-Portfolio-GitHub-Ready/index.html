04 / JOURNAL CLUB

Research in conversation.

Turning academic literature into accessible conversations about learning, skills and the future of work.

01

AI • ACADEMIC INTEGRITY • ASSESSMENT

AI-Generated Text Detection & Academic Integrity

A Journal Club discussion based on research into AI-generated text detection and the prevention of unethical AI use in student assessment. The session explored the limitations of AI detectors, the difference between AI generation and AI-assisted editing, and the challenge of protecting academic integrity without relying on detection tools as definitive proof.

Preview ↗View Journal Club 1 ↗
02

AI • ASSESSMENT DESIGN • MEANINGFUL LEARNING

Redefining Assessment in AI-Infused Learning

A research discussion examining how assessment may need to change as generative AI becomes embedded in education. The session considered authentic and process-based assessment, higher-order thinking, AI literacy, fairness, and how human–AI collaboration can support learning without replacing student thinking and judgement.

Preview ↗View Journal Club 2 ↗
03

GENERATIVE AI • INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN • COURSE DEVELOPMENT

Generative AI for Instructional Design

A Journal Club discussion examining how generative AI can support instructional designers during course planning and development. Based on a 2024 TechTrends study, the session explored ChatGPT’s ability to generate aligned learning objectives, activities and assessments, while also considering limitations such as weak contextual understanding, fabricated references and inconsistent quality.

The key takeaway was that AI can act as a rapid-prototyping and brainstorming partner, but instructional design expertise remains essential for evaluating, adapting and contextualising AI-generated material.
